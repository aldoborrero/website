**Aldo Borrero** is a consultant at [Numtide](https://numtide.com) who works on AI: coding agents, the tooling that lets them do real work, and the Nix and DevOps that make it reproducible. He writes about it here.

He has worked with LLMs since 2023 and builds open-source tools for coding agents: [ccq](https://github.com/aldoborrero/ccq) to search their history, [herdr-supervisor](https://github.com/aldoborrero/herdr-supervisor) to run them in isolated worktrees, and [the skills he uses with them](https://github.com/aldoborrero/pi-agent-kit). Through Numtide he has worked with Binarly on their Kubernetes infrastructure, with Golem Base (Arkiv) and PortalDeFi, and with other high-profile clients he can't name.

Before Numtide he co-founded 41North, a blockchain consultancy that built node infrastructure and MEV systems for clients such as Manifold Finance. He was lead engineer of [EthVM](https://github.com/EthVM/EthVM) at MyEtherWallet, built apps for AVG, Bayer and Worldreader at Mobile Jazz, and worked on the core banking back end at ING.
