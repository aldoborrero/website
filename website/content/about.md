**Aldo Borrero** is a consultant at [Numtide](https://numtide.com), where he works on Nix, DevOps and AI: reproducible infrastructure, and the tooling that lets coding agents do real work. He writes about it here.

Through Numtide he has worked with Binarly on their Kubernetes infrastructure, and with Golem Base and PortalDeFi. He builds open-source tools for coding agents, such as [ccq](https://github.com/aldoborrero/ccq) and [herdr-supervisor](https://github.com/aldoborrero/herdr-supervisor).

Before Numtide he co-founded 41North, a blockchain consultancy that built node infrastructure and MEV systems for clients such as Manifold Finance. He was lead engineer of [EthVM](https://github.com/EthVM/EthVM) at MyEtherWallet, built apps for AVG, Bayer and Worldreader at Mobile Jazz, and worked on the core banking back end at ING.
